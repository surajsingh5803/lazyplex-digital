Which properties and business situations fit

A new residential launch needs accurate project details and a reliable enquiry path. A developer with several ongoing projects needs a site architecture that helps buyers understand which configurations and localities are available. A brokerage with multiple listings needs a different approach to individual property inventory and listing expiry.

We begin by confirming which projects, localities, services and locations the business actually covers. The goal is not to manufacture a landing page for every neighbourhood and configuration. It is to help a buyer make a better-informed choice.

Developers

Focus on project discovery, approved details and direct sales-team handoffs.

Brokers

Prioritise current listings, transparent representation and available inventory.

Project groups

Use consistent navigation while keeping distinct projects differentiated.

Technical audit and information architecture

We review canonical URLs, XML sitemaps, indexing evidence from Search Console, internal links, mobile navigation, duplicate inventory and page retirement. A crawler's indexable flag is not proof that Google has indexed a page.

Project pages should identify the actual development, accurate location, permitted property details, available configurations, relevant amenities and how a buyer can contact the project. Approved documents or project registration details should be checked against the relevant authority before publication. We do not invent investment appreciation, possession dates or prices.

How we decide whether to create a locality page

A separate locality URL makes sense when it helps visitors compare an actual set of projects or discover information not covered elsewhere. Two pages with the same property listings and swapped area names create a poor experience.

Potentially useful differentiators include verified available projects, actual sales coverage, public transport or road-access sources, administrative context and buyer questions specific to that market. Avoid unsupported travel times, promises about future infrastructure or claims of guaranteed rental yield.

Measure qualified property search enquiries

Track branded versus non-branded project discovery, useful entry pages, enquiry button clicks and submitted forms where they are genuinely captured. Then work with sales teams to separate reachable prospects, qualified buyers, booked site visits, completed visits and bookings.

A person who clicks a call link, returns via Google Ads and later sends WhatsApp may create several digital interactions. We should not represent those as several unique customers. An agreed CRM stage definition makes cross-channel reports more honest.

Search signals

Priority page impressions, clicks and technical health.

Contact actions

Form, WhatsApp handoff and click-to-call event types kept separate.

Sales outcomes

Reachable, budget-fit buyer; site visit; booking where verified.

What a scoped SEO engagement includes

A typical first scope documents the priority project pages, technical findings, content approvals, internal-link fixes, project and location information to verify and relevant reporting definitions. Work requiring real-estate legal or RERA review is sent for client approval.

SEO can support high-intent project discovery but cannot guarantee positions or bookings. Media buying, fresh project photography, CRM implementation and extensive site reconstruction should be separately agreed when they are required.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a page for every residential locality?

No. Create distinct pages only where each provides current, verifiable local information and answers a different buyer decision.

Can an agency promise first-page rankings?

No. Rankings depend on search competition, quality, indexing and external factors. We agree on deliverables and measurable signals instead.

Is real estate Google Ads part of SEO?

No. Paid campaigns are a separate service, although project pages and lead definitions can be shared.
