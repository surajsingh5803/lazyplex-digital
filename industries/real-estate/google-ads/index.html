Begin with project facts and targeting permissions

Before launching, confirm approved project name, developer, location, current configurations, price disclosures and availability. The client should verify registration and promotional rules that apply to the property. A marketing team must not improvise inventory, possession dates or return-on-investment projections.

Google Ads policies for housing can restrict certain personalised targeting in applicable jurisdictions. Campaign requirements must be checked for the actual country and ad settings. We do not use a generic demographic exclusion rule without reviewing the relevant housing category policy.

Current project facts, assets and landing-page statements.

Country-specific ad rules and housing-targeting applicability.

Sales owner, response process and valid enquiry definition.

Choose campaign intent groups carefully

Keep a distinction between the project brand, searches for a residential unit in a location, broader research searches and competitor-related terms. Someone seeking directions is not necessarily ready to purchase; someone seeking an approved configuration and local project may be a stronger prospect.

A Search campaign offers a clearer way to inspect search terms and messaging. Performance Max may offer additional inventory but needs suitable assets and reliable conversion signals. We recommend one only where it fits the account and permitted policy settings.

Make the landing page earn the click

A relevant project landing page should show verified information, make it easy to compare available configurations and have an accessible enquiry path. If the advert says a particular location or configuration, the page should substantiate that information.

Track connected call and form outcomes where possible. A button press or WhatsApp handoff is not a booked site visit. Make sure the sales team can see which project and campaign produced each enquiry without transferring more personal information to analytics than necessary.

Avoid the common cost-per-lead trap

An apparent low cost per lead may conceal duplicates, students, unrelated contractors or buyers whose budget is far below the available inventory. Working with sales teams to classify reachable and project-fit buyers is essential before scaling spend.

The report should distinguish spend, platform conversions, unique reachable enquiries, qualified buyers, site visits scheduled, attended visits and bookings. Attribution may be incomplete across channels; avoid claiming every booking was created solely by Google Ads.

What is included in a paid-search review

A focused engagement can include account and conversion diagnosis, targeting and policy review, keyword themes, ad and landing-page approval, search-term hygiene, tracking definitions, and recurring quality review with the sales team.

Media spend and management fees must be separate in a proposal. A shared lead calculator can help agree on what constitutes a useful result before budget decisions are made.

Frequently asked questions

Should every property project use Performance Max?

No. The account's eligible inventory, creative assets, conversion measurement and goal should guide campaign choices.

Can we target only a particular demographic for housing?

Advertising restrictions may apply depending on the platform, location and housing classification. Review the current policy before configuring targeting.

Does a low CPL mean the property campaign is successful?

Not necessarily. Reachable, qualified buyers and attended visits can be more meaningful than cheap raw form submissions.
