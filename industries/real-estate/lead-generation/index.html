Real Estate Lead Generation That Looks Beyond Form Submissions

A large list of phone numbers is not a property sales pipeline. We help structure project marketing around who the buyer is, whether the project fits their needs and what happens between an enquiry and a site visit.

Identify the commercial goal before selecting channels

A property launch, unsold inventory in an established project and a luxury development have different prospective buyers and sales processes. First agree the project, approved offer, genuine availability, configuration, location and the minimum information a buyer needs to make a sensible enquiry.

A useful buyer-fit definition may include budget, preferred micro-market, configuration needs, purpose of purchase and decision timeframe. Those are inputs to a respectful sales conversation, not compulsory fields in every form. Avoid collecting unnecessary personal details.

New project launch

Introduce the approved project and explain who it suits.

Existing inventory

Answer availability, location and configuration questions precisely.

Sales backlog

Diagnose slow response and qualification before paying for more raw leads.

Build a lead journey that does not lose intent

An advert or search result should lead to the corresponding project, not a generic list of properties. The page should describe the project with approved facts, answer recurring questions and offer a reachable contact method. A shorter form may be appropriate when sales teams can qualify the rest in conversation.

When the site uses WhatsApp handoff, a click must be reported as an attempt to open messaging, not a delivered message. CRM ownership and follow-up play a major role: an unanswered relevant enquiry cannot become a site visit through better targeting alone.

Landing page

Verified offer, project fit, evidence and clear next step.

Enquiry intake

Reasonable fields, consent and duplicates review.

Sales handoff

Named owner, response discipline and staged follow-up.

Use a qualification funnel, not a single CPL

Report leads submitted, deduplicated contacts, reachable prospects, qualified buyers, scheduled site visits, attended visits and booked units when confirmed. Those stages can be tracked in CRM without publishing personal information.

Cost per lead is useful for early diagnosis but can conceal poor buyer fit. Cost per qualified lead, cost per attended site visit and cost per booking may be more informative when the sales team can consistently maintain those stages.

Lead

A submitted contact; can be incomplete or duplicated.

Qualified buyer

Verified fit for project location, budget and needs.

Sales outcome

Visit, booking and revenue where data is available.

Channel mix and project requirements

Search ads may capture clear project and locality intent. Social campaigns can build interest with suitable visual assets. Organic project and locality pages can help people research before contacting sales. The mix depends on inventory, permissible claims, budgets and the ability to respond.

For residential housing campaigns, relevant ad-platform targeting rules must be checked for the selected country and campaign settings. Project registration, promotional disclosures and approved pricing require client verification under applicable rules. We do not promise that a particular campaign will meet a quota of bookings.

What Lazy Plex would deliver

A scoped engagement can include qualification definitions, campaign and landing-page review, project form improvements, UTM and event taxonomy, CRM routing recommendations and stage reporting. Advertising spend, new website features and CRM installation are separately scoped as needed.

We recommend reviewing a sample of actual enquiries with the sales team, without exporting private buyer records into public reporting, and making changes to search intent, copy and follow-up based on what those conversations reveal.

Discovery

Validate project, target buyer and sales process.

Delivery

Improve matching pages and measurable enquiry routes.

Review

Report qualified stages and revise after sales feedback.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a qualified real estate lead?

A prospect who meets your agreed fit and intent criteria, rather than any form submission or call click.

Can Lazy Plex guarantee a fixed number of bookings?

No. Bookings depend on the offer, inventory, sales capacity and buyer circumstances, as well as marketing.

Do I need a new CRM first?

Not always. We first review what lead stages and handoffs can be measured using your existing process.
