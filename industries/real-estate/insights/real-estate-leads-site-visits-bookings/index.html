PROPERTY MARKETING FIELD GUIDE

How to Measure Real Estate Leads, Site Visits and Bookings

Marketing can look busy while the sales team struggles to find qualified buyers. A useful report distinguishes what happened online from the buyer conversations, attended visits and actual bookings recorded by sales.

Define the event before adding it to a dashboard

A click-to-call action is not proof of a connected telephone conversation. Opening WhatsApp is not a sent message. A form may be duplicate, incomplete or irrelevant to the project.

Report raw event types separately from verified unique contacts. Record which system produced each event and whether deduplication is possible. Avoid sharing unnecessary personal details in dashboards.

Use a stable sales-stage dictionary

Stages might include new, first contact attempted, reached, qualified buyer, site visit scheduled, site visit attended, booking initiated and booking confirmed. Choose stages that the actual sales team can maintain.

Scheduled and attended are different. Someone may cancel a visit or book another project. A CRM should allow stage and project changes without treating every interaction as a new customer.

Calculate conversion using consistent cohorts

Suppose a fictional month has 100 reachable contacts, 40 confirmed qualified buyers and 16 visits attended by that group. Qualification among reachable contacts is 40%. Attendance among those qualified buyers is 40% if all events are part of the same measured cohort.

Using mismatched calendar windows can distort the calculation because property buying decisions often take more than a day. Specify date ranges, cohort logic and whether visits in one month arose from contacts in a previous period.

Investigate where demand disappears

A large drop between submitted forms and reachable buyers suggests inaccurate information, poor form validation or source quality. A drop between visit scheduling and attendance may involve follow-up, timing, travel or availability.

Use a short sales-team review to identify the dominant cause before increasing advertising spend. Unverified lead scores should never be presented as certain buyer intent.

Build a scorecard the business can act on

Show channel, project, spend, raw interactions, unique reachable buyers, qualified buyers, scheduled visits, attended visits and bookings. Put assumptions and missing data alongside the figures.

The free real estate funnel calculator can test scenarios but cannot guarantee any forecast. Actual sales decisions should rely on maintained CRM records and the project's real economics.

Frequently asked questions

Is a scheduled site visit a completed site visit?

No. Track planned and attended visits separately.

Can one property buyer produce several lead events?

Yes. Multiple channels and repeated forms can refer to the same person.

Does a site visit count as a booking?

No. Bookings are a separate commercial outcome requiring verification.
