PRACTICAL PROPERTY MARKETING GUIDE

Search Ads vs Performance Max for Real Estate Campaigns

Campaign formats are tools, not strategies. The useful choice depends on whether the developer has verified inventory, enough approved creative assets and a reliable view of the enquiries that actually fit the project.

What Search campaigns offer

Google Search campaigns can reach users entering explicit search terms. A developer can organise messaging around valid project, configuration and location needs, then inspect search-term evidence and landing-page relevance.

Search does not automatically mean good buyer fit. Irrelevant broad queries, outdated inventory and conversion actions based only on link taps can still produce misleading results. Campaign structure needs review against actual sales feedback.

What Performance Max can add

Performance Max can use other Google inventory with automated optimisation. It requires suitable approved creative assets and a useful conversion signal. If the primary action is merely opening WhatsApp, the system may optimise for that tap rather than a serious property enquiry.

Where available, verified qualified-lead data can make campaign comparisons more meaningful. But reporting and channel attribution can differ, so evaluate it against the actual business stage you are trying to improve.

Housing ad rules require a real check

Housing promotions may have restrictions on personalised targeting depending on the platform rules and relevant jurisdiction. Google publishes specific guidance on housing targeting. The applicability to a particular campaign should be reviewed rather than assumed.

The developer must approve project registration details, price statements, configuration availability and other claims. A marketing team should not describe a project as approved, available or offering guaranteed returns without documented evidence.

How to decide which format to test

If the project has identifiable search demand, controlled messaging and a small budget, Search may provide a practical first experiment. Where the account has varied creative, healthy conversion volume and strong lead-quality feedback, Performance Max may be considered as a controlled addition.

Avoid launching multiple formats simply because competing agencies do. Agree on the hypothesis, eligible settings, source-of-truth lead stages and review cadence before allocating budget.

Look beyond platform conversions

Measure spend, relevant search activity, recorded contact actions, reachable prospects, qualified buyers, scheduled visits, attended visits and bookings, with clear definitions. A message-app handoff is not a delivered lead.

If quality drops, check the promise, the query or campaign asset, the landing page and the sales team's response before raising budget. No format guarantees units sold.

Frequently asked questions

Should every developer run Performance Max?

No. It depends on account conditions, conversion quality and suitable assets.

Can we restrict housing ad audiences in any way we choose?

No. Check current platform housing policy and jurisdiction-specific restrictions.

Does a WhatsApp handoff count as a qualified lead?

No. The message must actually be sent and the enquiry assessed.
