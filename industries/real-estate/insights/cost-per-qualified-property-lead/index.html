Real Estate Cost per Lead vs Cost per Qualified Buyer

A lower cost per lead does not always mean a property campaign is reaching buyers who can afford the project or are likely to attend a site visit. Measuring the next stages reveals where the actual value is.

CPL is a starting point, not the final verdict

Cost per lead (CPL) divides campaign spend by recorded lead events. Cost per qualified lead (CPQL) divides the same spend by enquiries that satisfy an agreed buyer-fit definition. The second number is often higher, but that does not mean the campaign became less effective.

Raw contact events can include duplicated forms, missed calls, accidental taps and requests outside project availability. Preserve those interaction counts separately from verified unique buyers so the report remains auditable.

Worked example using illustrative figures

Assume a fictional project spends ₹60,000 and captures 150 submitted enquiries. CPL is ₹400. After deduplication and sales review, suppose 60 are reachable and genuinely relevant to the available project. CPQL is ₹1,000. If 20 attend a site visit, advertising cost per attended visit is ₹3,000.

These numbers are illustrations, not Lazy Plex client results or market benchmarks. They exclude management fees and sales costs. A valid comparison must clearly state the period, campaign spend included and the operational meaning of each lead stage.

Define qualification with the sales team

A sensible qualification conversation can check project location, real budget, desired configuration, intent and timeframe. Not every question belongs in a long initial form; the sales team may obtain some details during appropriate follow-up.

One phone number can represent a family, while the same person can submit several forms. A deduplication rule needs a lookback period and room for human review. Avoid exporting unnecessary buyer personal details into public dashboards.

Why site visits change the analysis

A scheduled site visit is not an attended visit. A visit is not a booking. The funnel should show new contacts, reached prospects, qualified buyers, visits scheduled, visits attended and bookings separately.

If many buyers are qualified but do not attend, the sales confirmation or availability process may be the problem. If very few reached buyers qualify, review ad targeting, page content and the advertised project's fit before increasing spend.

Compare channels without claiming perfect attribution

Buyers may see a social advert, search the developer and contact sales on WhatsApp. Several channels can contribute to one enquiry; do not attribute the entire outcome several times.

Choose one consistent operational attribution rule, document what it misses and review CRM data with the team. The free property-funnel calculator can model assumptions but cannot guarantee site visits or bookings.

Frequently asked questions

Is the cheapest CPL campaign the best?

Not necessarily; check reachable buyers, qualification and attended site visits.

Should we delete duplicate form events?

Keep event counts for auditing and separately report verified unique enquiries.

Is CPQL the same as customer acquisition cost?

No. Acquisition cost needs a defined customer outcome and all relevant costs.
