Google Ads for Hospitals and Clinics: Build Campaigns Around Real Enquiries

Healthcare paid search requires more than switching on a campaign. Advertising eligibility, medical claims, locations, landing pages and the reception team's definition of a valid enquiry must be considered before a budget is scaled.

THE APPROACH

Paid acquisition with clear compliance, intent and measurement guardrails. The material here describes possible work and decision criteria, not a guaranteed result or an undisclosed client case study.

Start with an account and eligibility review

We review the service being advertised, target location, advertiser status, relevant healthcare advertising policies, existing conversion definitions and actual landing pages. Some healthcare services and claims may be restricted by country or require additional certification. We do not describe every treatment as automatically eligible.

The campaign should state only what the hospital or clinic can confirm. We exclude unsupported cure, outcome or recovery promises and recommend review by the authorised medical and compliance team before launch.

Further reading: Google Ads healthcare and medicines policy · General Google Ads management.

Match campaign groups to patient intent—not just speciality names

The page a person reaches must answer the need indicated by the search. A clinic-name search, a doctor's consultation search and a general question about a treatment are not interchangeable. Use appropriate ad groups and landing pages for legitimate, permitted intent and avoid mixing brand demand with new prospect acquisition when reporting performance.

Clinic or brand searches

Direct users to accurate opening hours, locations and booking routes; report brand-driven demand separately.

Speciality consultation searches

Land on a verified speciality page with practitioner, service scope and appointment guidance.

Research-stage questions

Offer educational content where appropriate, not an aggressive appointment form on every informational search.

Search Ads, Performance Max and budget decisions

Search campaigns allow direct review of the terms and messages used to meet a defined search intent. Performance Max can use additional inventory and automated optimisation, but its suitability depends on conversion quality, asset availability, policy eligibility and sufficient data. Neither format is automatically right for every clinic.

We recommend setting a test hypothesis, daily budgets and lead-review cadence before scaling. A low reported cost per conversion can be misleading if a large share of calls were accidental or unrelated; reconcile platform conversions with the clinic's real contact outcomes.

Practical sequence

  1. Confirm service and advertiser eligibility for the target market.
  2. Audit tracking and distinguish primary from secondary conversion actions.
  3. Prepare approved landing pages and service-specific ad messages.
  4. Launch with appropriate geographic and search-intent controls.
  5. Review search terms, invalid contacts and appointment quality with the operations team.
  6. Refine spend using qualified stages instead of raw conversion volume.

Patient enquiry quality and reception feedback

A form filled twice by the same person is not two new patients. A call click may not connect, a directions request may not become a consultation, and a booked appointment may not be attended. We label those stages and avoid claiming that Google Ads reports unique patients by default.

When operational teams can provide aggregated lead feedback, the report can compare spend, valid new enquiries, appointments requested and confirmed visits. Sensitive patient or diagnosis information should not be exposed in campaign measurement systems.

Further reading: Hospital lead counting method · Clinic appointment-quality guide.

What we scope and report

A focused package may cover account diagnosis, campaign architecture, suitable keyword themes and negatives, approved ad assets, landing-page improvements, conversion tracking review and reporting. We identify which inputs the clinic must supply and what work needs medical or legal approval.

Media spend and agency fees are separate in any proposal. We recommend budget changes only with reference to the agreed campaign goal, available feedback and limitations of the data.

Healthcare Google Ads FAQs

Can all healthcare treatments be advertised on Google?

No. Eligibility depends on the specific content, location, advertiser certifications and current policies. It must be reviewed case by case.

Should clinics use Performance Max rather than Search?

Not as a blanket rule. The choice depends on account data, assets, lead-quality tracking and permitted inventory.

Do you promise a cost per patient?

No. We define valid leads and distinguish completed consultations from online conversion events before making projections.

Can Google Ads work without a new website?

Sometimes, if existing pages accurately answer the ad and have usable contact routes. We assess them before recommending a rebuild.
