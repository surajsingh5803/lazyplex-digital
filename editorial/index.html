TRANSPARENCY & CONTENT QUALITY

How we research, review and update our guides.

Lazy Plex Digital publishes practical information to help businesses choose marketing services responsibly. This page explains the editorial standards we aim to follow, what our content can and cannot establish, and how readers can flag corrections.

Who is responsible for this website?

Lazy Plex operates digital.lazyplex.com. Suraj Kumar is the founder of Lazy Plex; the website's published guides use Lazy Plex as the organisational author unless a specific contributor has been identified and has reviewed the content. Founder identity is not a claim that every article was written by the founder personally.

The content is intended for business decision-makers, marketers, operators and founders evaluating SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, websites, conversion systems and related services. For a particular engagement, contact Lazy Plex to clarify what can be delivered.

What we aim to verify before publication

Whenever a page makes factual claims about a platform feature, official guidance or measurement definition, we aim to check the relevant primary source and distinguish that guidance from our own editorial recommendations. Pages may link to official platform documentation and relevant source material. Terms, product policies and marketing-platform interfaces can change: a linked source should be checked again when making important decisions.

Industry pages should address the actual purchase or enquiry process, not repeat the same service description with different business labels. A healthcare website needs a different enquiry framework from a property project's site-visit funnel or a manufacturer's technical RFQ process. We aim to make these differences clear in the headings, examples and recommended measurements.

How examples and numbers are labelled

Illustrative arithmetic examples are hypothetical. They are useful to explain formulas such as cost per qualified lead, contribution margin, conversion rates and acquisition costs. They are not client results, independently verified benchmarks or promises of future performance. When numerical examples are fictional, we label them as such.

Client case studies require permission, accurate underlying evidence and enough context to understand the measures. We distinguish click-to-call events from verified calls, form submissions from qualified enquiries, and enquiry volume from appointments, site visits or closed sales. We do not publish a named client's confidential financial, healthcare or campaign information without the right to do so.

AI assistance and human responsibility

AI-assisted research, drafting and coding tools may be used in preparing this website. Their output is not evidence by itself. We aim to review important technical and commercial claims, avoid fabricating first-hand experience, and correct unsupported statements. Where an article is authored by the organisation, it does not imply it is a manually written personal diary or a signed expert opinion.

For search and AI visibility, we focus on crawlable pages, clear answers, transparent methodology, accurate author/publisher identification and genuinely useful resources. A robots.txt rule or optional AI navigation file does not guarantee inclusion in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews or other services.

Healthcare, financial and regulated subjects

Marketing content for regulated professions has extra responsibilities. Our healthcare marketing guides discuss acquisition methods and patient enquiry systems; they are not medical advice, nor are they a substitute for a clinician's review, privacy assessment or the advertising rules that apply to a provider. Advice on acquisition cost, budget and ROAS is similarly informational and not a guarantee of revenue, profitability or tax treatment.

How we handle service recommendations

Lazy Plex is a commercial service provider. The content can refer to our own SEO, advertising, website, lead-generation or analytics services when genuinely relevant to a reader's question. Such links are commercial context, not independent third-party endorsements. We avoid claims that a business can reach a certain ranking, lead volume or return on spend without an appropriate factual basis.

We prefer a clear choice between a focused individual service and a wider growth engagement. Service scope, platform access, responsibility for approvals, reporting definitions and fees should be documented before work begins. Readers can also explore the delivery and reporting approach and the service directory.

How content is maintained and corrected

Pages are reviewed when factual inaccuracies, broken references or material policy changes come to attention. Last-modified dates, when displayed, should reflect meaningful edits rather than cosmetic automation. We do not claim every page is reviewed daily or that every marketing platform's rules remain unchanged.

If you find a correction, a source that no longer supports a claim, an accessibility issue or a privacy concern, send us a message or call +91 72579 19567. Include the page URL and the section concerned without submitting sensitive personal information. We will evaluate the evidence before updating the page.

Useful tools and references

Try our free lead quality and marketing ROI calculator for transparent funnel arithmetic, and browse industry marketing guides. For Google's public content guidance, consult the helpful, reliable, people-first content guidance. For advertising conversion definitions see Google Ads' qualified and converted lead guidance.
