# Lazy Plex AI Search — manual visibility observation plan

Created: 16 November 2026. This is an **unrun measurement protocol**, not a report of AI citations, brand mentions, ranking or market share. OpenSEO AI visibility tracker is not configured for this project as of this date. No paid run or recurring credit-consuming schedule has been started.

## Why use a fixed prompt sample?

AI answers differ by engine, date, phrasing, country and prompt. Treat each observation as one answer in a documented sample; do not infer total visibility from a few prompts. Only a meaningful citation to a real Lazy Plex URL should count as a *citation*. An answer merely mentioning the name is a separate signal. Do not insert Lazy Plex's name into the neutral queries below.

## Recording columns

Date (UTC), engine/product, country, exact prompt, branded/unbranded, brand mentioned (yes/no), exact source URLs cited, valid Lazy Plex citation (yes/no), type of mention, observation notes, reviewer. Keep the full response text only where allowed by the tool's terms and the user's privacy requirements.

## Review rules

1. Run the same prompts on the same engine and locale at agreed intervals. Do not secretly replace queries to favour the brand.
2. Separately count an explicit brand name mention and a link citation to digital.lazyplex.com or lazyplex.com. Record third-party sources neutrally.
3. Record errors, omitted citations and no-answer responses rather than discarding them.
4. Use the observed dates, not the publishing dates of underlying content, to compare results.
5. Treat possible AI-assisted visits or enquiry sources as supplemental to reliable business analytics, not a substitute for verified qualified leads.
6. No citation purchases, synthetic review campaigns, made-up experts or artificial “AI ranking” guarantees.

After a connected tracker is configured and any estimated credit spend is approved, OpenSEO can gather comparable observations. Until then the outcome is **not measured**, not zero.
