OUR APPROACH · FROM FIRST CONVERSATION TO MEANINGFUL REPORTING

How Lazy Plex approaches a digital marketing project

Start with a real business problem, choose the right work, confirm the facts and measure what actually happens after a customer enquires. This is the process we propose—not a guarantee of rankings, leads or revenue.

How we approach work

1. Start with a specific business problem

One business may have low search visibility; another may have plenty of enquiries but poor sales response. Buying the same set of services will not solve both problems. A first conversation should identify what the organisation offers, which customers it can legitimately serve and the decision it wants visitors to make.

Where there is access, the review can include existing website pages, search visibility, ad account setup, profile accuracy, enquiry handling and available CRM stages. If access or reliable numbers are missing, that gap is recorded rather than filled with assumptions.

The starting deliverable is a concise scope: the problem, likely constraints, what requires client approval, what can be implemented and which outcome can be measured. A diagnosis is not a promise that a particular channel will produce a certain number of leads.

2. Choose a focused first service

Search engine optimisation helps customers discover accurate, useful pages. Paid search can connect current buying intent to a relevant offer. Website or conversion work may be the immediate need when visitors cannot find contact details or complete a form. CRM or follow-up improvements can matter when the sales team cannot respond consistently.

We favour choosing a clear first workstream instead of adding channels simply because they are available. The initial plan should explain what is included, what is excluded, the responsible people, required access, approval steps and a sensible time to review progress.

For more complex organisations, several services may eventually work together. The business should still be able to understand which part of the programme is addressing which problem.

3. Verify factual claims and responsibilities

Healthcare, property, education and other regulated or high-trust industries need factual review. A marketing team can help with content structure, readability and search relevance, but cannot invent doctor credentials, treatment outcomes, property registrations, prices, availability or institutional approvals.

Before publication, relevant owners should confirm service offerings, locations, policies, photographs, brand assets and any outcome claims. New client case studies, awards and testimonials are published only after evidence and permission are available. When proof is not available, we describe methods and decision frameworks instead.

Advertising can have rules that vary by product, target country and audience category. Eligibility and approved promotional claims must be checked before a campaign is launched.

4. Build, link and test the customer journey

A website page should have a distinct purpose, helpful content, accessible headings and a clear way to take the next step. Internal links ought to explain meaningful relationships between services, industries and practical guides rather than forming a wall of keywords.

Technical checks can examine the page title, description, canonical URL, link targets, crawlability, relevant structured data, JavaScript errors and mobile interaction. A passed code test demonstrates those checks ran; it does not guarantee search rankings, accessibility in every environment or inclusion in Google's index.

For an enquiry flow, test the journey from the originating page through the contact form and into the appropriate team. For example, opening WhatsApp is only a handoff; the visitor must still send the prepared message before an enquiry is delivered.

5. Measure activity and verified outcomes separately

Traffic, impressions, phone-link taps, direction requests and form submissions can indicate discovery or interest, but they are not automatically distinct new customers. A buyer may use several channels, return to the site and contact a business more than once.

Where the business can confirm downstream stages, define unique reachable contacts, qualified enquiries, booked appointments or site visits, attendance and commercial outcomes separately. The right funnel depends on the industry and the client's operational process. Respect privacy: avoid unnecessary medical or other sensitive information in marketing analytics.

Reports should identify the source, period and definition of each metric, including uncertainty or missing information. If campaign activity cannot be connected reliably to booked revenue, it should not be reported as verified return on investment.

6. Review, improve and keep an audit trail

Some changes can be evaluated quickly for technical correctness, such as whether a page loads or a link resolves. Search demand and lead quality often require more time and a stable measurement method. Adjustments should follow reliable observations rather than unverified claims of instant success.

A useful review describes completed work, outstanding approvals, operational feedback and the next highest-priority action. For SEO work, that can include technical fixes, index coverage, useful search queries and content accuracy. For paid acquisition, it may include eligible targeting, valid conversions and follow-up quality.

We also distinguish an implemented change from a published and externally verified one. A successful GitHub deployment, for example, does not by itself show that Google has indexed or ranked the page.

7. Know what we need from each other

Lazy Plex can review marketing assets, prepare a proposed service scope, implement agreed changes and document what was checked. The client retains ownership of its factual business information, access permissions, regulated claims, sales follow-up and the business decisions that depend on those inputs.

An initial project brief is more useful when it includes the current website or campaign, the actual service or project, target customer, legitimate locations, the main bottleneck and who will evaluate enquiries. Confidential performance information or client names are not prerequisites for a first conversation.

If the best first step is to repair a contact route or clarify a service page, we should say so—even when that is smaller than a full marketing retainer.
