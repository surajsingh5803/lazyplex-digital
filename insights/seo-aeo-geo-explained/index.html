SEO vs AEO vs GEO: What Businesses Actually Need

Search, answer engines and AI-generated answers are changing how people discover companies. But many promises about “AI ranking hacks” do not match how Google says its AI search features work.

SEO remains the foundation

Search engine optimisation helps people and search systems find, understand and evaluate useful pages. Technical accessibility, real service information, internal links, credible sources and accurate metadata remain important.

Google's AI optimisation guidance says its AI search features rely on existing core Search ranking and quality systems. A website with indexing problems or thin content cannot fix them by simply renaming its SEO package GEO.

What businesses often mean by AEO

Answer engine optimisation describes making accurate answers to customer questions easier to find and understand. A genuinely helpful explanation, precise product or service details and appropriate markup can be useful.

It does not mean creating hundreds of superficial FAQs or treating schema markup as a guarantee of citation. The content should answer a real question in context and match what the business actually does.

How GEO is usually positioned

Generative engine optimisation focuses on whether AI-generated answers can find or mention a company, concept or source. That goal overlaps strongly with discoverable, authoritative and original content.

Google has specifically advised against special “chunking” for its AI search features, unnecessary AI text files and inauthentic mentions. Unique evidence, clear source context and existing SEO practices offer a more credible starting point.

Measure visibility honestly

A practical review can define a set of buyer questions, record the search or AI product used, date, prompt and whether a relevant website was cited. Sampling should be repeated consistently and its limits stated.

A handful of manually checked answers is not a precise share of AI visibility. Combine observations with available Search Console signals, valid referral data and qualified enquiries rather than turning uncertain mentions into a fabricated percentage.

Choose an order of operations

First verify indexability and crawl access. Then improve the strongest commercial pages, provide original expertise and verifiable company facts, and connect useful guides with appropriate internal links.

After that, monitor a defined set of search and AI answers, note what is and is not measurable and adjust content where it actually helps customers. No agency can guarantee mention in a particular AI assistant or generated answer.

Frequently asked questions

Does AEO replace SEO?

No. Useful answers and technical search foundations overlap heavily.

Do we need llms.txt for Google AI Overviews?

Google does not require it for inclusion in its AI search features.

Can an agency guarantee AI citations?

No. Responses and citations vary across products, prompts and time.
