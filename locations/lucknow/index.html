Clinic and doctor discovery

For an independent specialist or a clinic, begin with accurate working hours, approved qualifications, real consultation locations and a route for requesting an appointment. Individual Google Business Profiles are subject to eligibility criteria; creating duplicates to cover more services can mislead patients.

A good reporting setup separates profile calls, direction requests, website phone taps, actual enquiries and attended consultations where the clinic can share aggregate outcomes. We do not present every platform interaction as a new patient.

Property project marketing and site visits

Residential property enquiries can be numerous but poorly qualified when the advertised project information and price range do not match the buyer. A project-specific landing page should explain the verified location, available configurations, approved details and next sales action.

Measure reachability, buyer fit, visits scheduled and attended. If an enquiry is outside budget or a visit is missed, the sales handoff needs review before adding more advertising.

Useful next steps: Real estate lead generation · Property funnel calculator.

Education and admissions enquiries

A college or training provider should make its actual programme, eligibility, fees and approved application steps easy to find. A generic “apply now” campaign can produce irrelevant applications when the course page does not answer fundamental questions.

Use a clear counselling and admissions stage dictionary: interested contact, eligible applicant, counselling, completed application and enrolment. The institution must verify its own educational claims and deadlines.

Useful next steps: Education marketing hub · Admission lead-generation guide.

Which digital service should come first?

For a clinic with incorrect profiles, local information accuracy may matter more than new paid media. For a developer with many enquiries but few visits, qualification and CRM handoff may be the immediate priority. A business without a usable website should fix its main contact path before spending to send more people there.

We review the existing assets, who handles enquiries and what a successful outcome means. Services are available individually: SEO, paid search, landing-page improvement, lead generation and automation can be scoped independently.
