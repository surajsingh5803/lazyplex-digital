How to read this: Required leads are rounded up. This is a reverse-planning scenario, not a prediction of demand or conversion. If the result is negative, your contribution assumptions do not cover the modelled marketing cost.

Method: Qualified leads = ceil(customer target ÷ closing rate). Raw leads = ceil(qualified leads ÷ qualification rate). Advertising spend = raw leads × assumed raw CPL. All-in budget = advertising spend + other marketing costs. Contribution after marketing = customers × contribution per customer − all-in budget.

What should you validate before using this as a budget?

The estimates depend entirely on your qualification rate, closing rate, cost per raw lead and unit contribution. Use real business data when available, consider channel constraints, time to conversion, duplicate enquiries and delivery capacity. A mathematical target is not proof that this number of leads can be purchased at the entered cost.

This tool does not collect or transmit your inputs. For a forward-looking analysis using already-observed spend and leads, use the Lead Quality & ROI Calculator. For property-specific site-visit stages use the Real Estate Funnel Calculator.
