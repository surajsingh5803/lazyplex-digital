How to interpret the figures

Use real CRM stage counts where possible. A website click, a WhatsApp handoff or a submitted form is not automatically a unique buyer. Scheduled visits and attended visits are different outcomes. Keep cost inputs and date ranges consistent.

Media spend alone does not include the full cost of customer acquisition. For a complete commercial analysis, account for fees, team costs, project margins and attribution limitations separately.
