## Cross-industry editorial safeguards

- Google explicitly does **not** require a fixed word count. The 3,500–5,500-word pillar target is the requested editorial brief, not a Google ranking factor.
- Replace any plausible keyword hypothesis with Search Console / Keyword Planner data as it becomes available. Do not claim volume, ranking, CPC or guaranteed conversions without evidence.
- Review each page for actual service scope, current platform rules, market eligibility, and inaccurate generalisations before public promotion.
- For healthcare, aesthetic medicine, finance or legally regulated professional services, obtain qualified factual and regulatory review before publishing any sensitive claims.
- Do not publish invented case studies, testimonials, partner credentials or client numerical outcomes. No such claims were added in this package.
- Word count and source list are provided in `CONTENT-QA.md` and `content-manifest.json`.
