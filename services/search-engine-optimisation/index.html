SEO SERVICE DETAILS

SEO services built around pages that can earn business

Our first question is not how many keywords a website can mention; it is which buying decisions the existing pages answer well. An SEO engagement can focus on technical discovery, page quality, a stronger commercial information architecture or clearer measurement. The work is scoped to the problems we can verify.

What a focused engagement covers

  1. Technical and indexation diagnosis: Review crawl access, canonicals, sitemap coverage, mobile usability, redirects and prominent internal paths. We distinguish crawler findings from Google indexation evidence.
  2. Keyword-to-page architecture: Assign one primary search intent to each important page. Compare live search results, identify overlaps and favour upgrading a strong existing URL over creating near-duplicate pages.
  3. Useful commercial content: Improve how services are explained: fit, deliverables, process, decision criteria, pricing factors and practical examples. Create genuinely distinct supporting content when it answers a separate question.
  4. Reporting and iteration: Track qualified organic enquiries alongside non-branded queries, clicks, impressions and landing-page engagement. Use results to revise the next set of pages rather than promising fixed ranking timelines.

How to decide if this is the right service

Begin with the business problem and the available data. We assess existing assets and the enquiry journey before recommending a standalone engagement or a connected project.

Best next step: share the target audience, locations or markets, current enquiry sources, approval constraints and one business outcome you want to improve.

Scope and pricing: complexity, locations, existing assets, implementation needs and review responsibilities affect a proposal; we do not publish fabricated universal pricing or guarantee rankings or leads.

Frequently asked questions

When can SEO begin generating leads?
It depends on current visibility, competition, technical health and conversion paths. We prioritise work by evidence, not a universal ranking deadline.

Do you create a page for every city and keyword variation?
No. We recommend separate pages only for genuinely different intent or materially useful local content.

What is included in an SEO proposal?
The agreed audit, priorities, changes, approvals, reporting cadence and exclusions should be written into the scope. Content and development needs vary by site.
