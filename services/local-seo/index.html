LOCAL SEO SERVICE DETAILS

Local SEO for businesses that need calls, visits and bookings

Local SEO is not only a Google Business Profile edit. The goal is to connect the searches people make in a particular area with accurate business information, a useful service page and a working enquiry route. A clinic with separate eligible practitioner profiles needs a different structure from a service-area business or a multi-branch retailer.

What a focused engagement covers

  1. Discovery and eligibility: Review the existing Google Business Profile inventory, permitted profile types, categories, access, landing pages and locations. We do not recommend duplicate or ineligible practitioner listings.
  2. Page and profile accuracy: Align business names, service descriptions, opening hours, appointment links, contact details and location-specific pages to confirmed information. Fix relevant mobile enquiry friction.
  3. Reputation and local relevance: Create a practical, policy-compliant review-response and profile-update workflow; map local search questions to useful service content rather than repeating city names.
  4. Reporting that means something: Separate GBP calls, directions, website clicks and form or WhatsApp enquiries. Where possible, match enquiries to appointments or qualified leads without treating every interaction as a new customer.

How to decide if this is the right service

Begin with the business problem and the available data. We assess existing assets and the enquiry journey before recommending a standalone engagement or a connected project.

Best next step: share the target audience, locations or markets, current enquiry sources, approval constraints and one business outcome you want to improve.

Scope and pricing: complexity, locations, existing assets, implementation needs and review responsibilities affect a proposal; we do not publish fabricated universal pricing or guarantee rankings or leads.

Frequently asked questions

Does a local SEO package include Google Ads?
No. Local SEO and paid ads are separate services. We can scope them together if your audience and budget make that useful.

Can you guarantee placement in the Google Maps three-pack?
No agency can guarantee a Maps position. Results depend on relevance, distance, prominence, competition and changing search conditions.

How do you evaluate progress?
Start with profile eligibility and page accuracy, then evaluate visibility, relevant interactions and qualified enquiries. We agree on definitions before reporting.
