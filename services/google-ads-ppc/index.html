PAID SEARCH SERVICE DETAILS

Google Ads that connect search intent to real outcomes

A click or automated conversion is not automatically a qualified lead. Our paid-search approach begins with the economics of the service or product, the searches that indicate intent and the point at which an enquiry is reviewed by the sales or reception team. Campaign types are selected to fit the objective, not because one format is universally better.

What a focused engagement covers

  1. Account and conversion review: Inspect conversion events, consent handling, geography, scheduling, landing-page relevance, campaign settings and existing search terms before recommending spend changes.
  2. Search and Performance Max choices: Choose campaign architecture according to available data, creative assets and buyer behaviour. Separate branded demand from new acquisition and document exclusions when supported.
  3. Landing-page and lead-quality work: Match the ad promise to a specific product, project, treatment or service; make the contact action clear and avoid misleading medical or property claims.
  4. Performance conversations: Report spend, valid enquiries, qualified stages and cost per qualified opportunity. Review search-term quality and feedback from the team handling enquiries.

How to decide if this is the right service

Begin with the business problem and the available data. We assess existing assets and the enquiry journey before recommending a standalone engagement or a connected project.

Best next step: share the target audience, locations or markets, current enquiry sources, approval constraints and one business outcome you want to improve.

Scope and pricing: complexity, locations, existing assets, implementation needs and review responsibilities affect a proposal; we do not publish fabricated universal pricing or guarantee rankings or leads.

Frequently asked questions

Is the advertising budget included in your fee?
Ad spend and management fees are separate decisions. Any proposal must make the media budget, work scope and third-party costs explicit.

Will you use Search Ads or Performance Max?
We first review the service, conversions and account data. The appropriate combination depends on measurement quality and campaign goals.

How do you reduce accidental or low-quality enquiries?
Review search terms, match types, negatives, location settings, landing pages and downstream feedback. No setting eliminates all accidental leads.
