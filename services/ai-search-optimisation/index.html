AI Search Optimisation Built on Credible SEO, Not AI Visibility Tricks

People increasingly discover companies through traditional search, AI-generated search summaries and conversational answers. Our approach strengthens the information those systems can access and evaluate—without claiming a secret shortcut to AI citations.

What businesses are actually buying

An organisation seeking AI visibility usually needs to know whether search systems can discover its useful pages, whether the services are explained accurately and whether trustworthy references support its claims. The first step is a search and content review, not a promise to get named in every chatbot answer.

AI search optimisation overlaps heavily with good SEO: clear technical access, useful first-hand content, descriptive titles, working internal links, accurate structured data where appropriate and genuine reputation. Google explicitly advises focusing on core SEO rather than specialised hacks for its AI features.

Technical SEO remains the foundation

We check indexing evidence, crawled page quality, headings, canonical configuration, accessible HTML, business details, breadcrumbs and meaningful links. Structured data must describe content that actually exists; there is no universal “AI ranking schema” that guarantees an appearance.

Google says its AI features use the existing core search and quality systems. That means no separate guaranteed submission pathway. If the website struggles with indexation or largely repetitive pages, those problems should be addressed before investing in experimental AI discovery tactics.

Publish information that deserves to be referenced

Useful content can include real product or service comparisons, accurately documented methods, author context, transparent assumptions, distinct FAQs and original research. Repeating synthetic marketing copy across multiple domains is not evidence of experience.

A case study should explain the situation, permitted observations, work performed and actual measurement limits. Never fabricate clients, rankings, endorsements, citations or author credentials to make the business appear authoritative.

Business pages

Answer what is offered, where, to whom and with which constraints.

Research assets

Show your methods and caveats so others can evaluate them.

Support pages

Address actual buyer questions, not invented keyword variations.

What to measure, and what not to promise

Measurement may include non-branded search visibility, relevant referring domains, crawl/indexing health, qualified enquiry paths and a documented sample of AI-answer mentions or citations for selected business questions.

AI-generated responses can vary across prompts, users and time. A small manual mention sample is not a population-level visibility percentage. We report the exact sampled questions, dates, observed citations and limitations rather than a fabricated market-share number.

Search metrics

Query groups, clicks, impressions and relevant entry pages.

Sampled AI answers

Documented query set, observation date and cited URLs.

Business outcomes

Enquiries or sales opportunities confirmed through normal channels.

A sensible first-phase engagement

First, confirm search access and indexation. Second, decide which existing commercial pages truly answer customer questions. Third, strengthen approved credentials, useful detail and verified examples. Fourth, connect related content and ensure contact routes work. Finally, establish a modest, transparent AI-answer sample alongside conventional SEO reporting.

We do not recommend mandatory AI text files, artificial “chunking” solely for AI systems, or purchasing fabricated brand mentions. Google directly advises against treating such practices as shortcuts to its AI features. The same core content principles also help visitors arriving from non-AI channels.

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee citations in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?

No. Each platform decides how to retrieve and present information, and responses vary.

Is AEO or GEO different from SEO?

They can describe broader discovery goals, but the practical foundation remains technically accessible, reliable, useful content and genuine authority.

Do we need an llms.txt file to appear in Google's AI features?

Google does not require one. We prioritise core SEO and content quality rather than unproven technical shortcuts.
