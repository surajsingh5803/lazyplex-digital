1. Confirm each industry offering is genuinely serviceable, with verified capabilities and truthful scope.
2. Editorially review articles for non-specific language; replace with firsthand evidence, real case material and search data when available.
3. Validate location-specific demand and prioritise high-value queries with Keyword Planner, Search Console and observed SERPs.
4. Have appropriately qualified reviewers verify medical and regulated industry content where necessary.
5. Do not claim fixed SEO ranking outcomes or search volumes. Content is not proof of market demand.

7. If domain HTTPS is still provisioning, do not promote the live website until the certificate is valid.
