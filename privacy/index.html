YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 19 November 2026. This notice describes the contact experience on digital.lazyplex.com.

Who operates this website?

Lazy Plex operates this marketing website. To discuss a privacy question, call or message +91 72579 19567.

What information do you choose to provide?

If you use the enquiry form, you may type your name, phone, email, business details, services required, marketing budget and message. Those details are used to compose a WhatsApp message in your browser.

How does the enquiry form work?

By default the form prepares a WhatsApp draft in your browser. Nothing is sent to Lazy Plex via WhatsApp until you choose to send it there. WhatsApp has its own privacy terms.

We are preparing an optional secure enquiry route through LP CRM. This option is displayed only if the protected CRM endpoint is enabled. If you explicitly select it, the submitted name, contact information, company, chosen service, budget and message are stored in the private CRM to reply to your request; authorised Lazy Plex staff can review them. The server applies spam, consent and rate-limit checks. The check may load Cloudflare Turnstile, which processes technical information under its own privacy practices. If the secure option is unavailable or the submission is not confirmed, the existing WhatsApp path remains available. Please do not share medical or other sensitive personal information in marketing enquiries.

Other technical information and referral labels

This site is hosted by a third-party static hosting provider that may process technical request data such as IP address and browser information to provide the service. The design also loads web fonts from Google, which may receive technical request information.

When a visitor arrives with recognised short campaign labels (UTM source, medium or campaign), the browser may keep those labels in this tab's session storage for up to 30 minutes. This helps carry a referral source to the contact form without keeping names, phone numbers, medical details or form messages in session storage. The labels may be shown in the WhatsApp draft, and you decide whether to send that message. Closing the tab ends that session storage.

The website records privacy-safe data-layer events for contact CTA clicks, telephone and WhatsApp clicks, WhatsApp handoff attempts and, when enabled, CRM-confirmed enquiry receipts. A click or handoff is not a delivered message or sales-qualified lead. We offer optional Google Analytics 4 (GA4), which loads only after you accept the analytics choice. If you reject, GA4 is not loaded or given your events. If you accept, Google may process visit statistics, approximate location and device or browser information and store analytics cookies under its own policies. We do not send contact names, phone numbers, email addresses or message text through our custom analytics events. GA4 reporting uses a shared Lazy Plex property; site traffic can be distinguished by hostname. Your optional choice is stored on this site for up to 180 days and can be changed using the 'Analytics preferences' control in the website footer. Advertising personalisation is disabled in our GA4 setup.

How information is used after you contact us

If you contact Lazy Plex by call or WhatsApp, the information you share may be used to respond to your enquiry, discuss services and manage ongoing communication. You can request that the team stop contacting you or ask about information you shared using the same phone number.

External links

Pages may link to the main Lazy Plex website or WhatsApp. Third-party websites have their own privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this page when website features, integrations or practices change.
